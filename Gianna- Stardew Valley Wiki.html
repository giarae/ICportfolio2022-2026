Gianna is an immortal thrust into the body of an eighteen-year-old human, since its inception. Her immortal soul affects the body such that it ages extremely slowly, which is why an untrained eye woud always mistake her as fifteen.

More Information

It is worthy to note that Gianna does not have memories of her previous life. At present, she tries to blend in with her peers—navigating friendships, love, recreation, and the college life in addition to the human adult world of taxes, job hunting, public transport, and bank accounts.

She struggles frequently with fitting in with her peers and social norms, which then creates feelings of alienation. During these moments, she questions if she is actually normal. However, these thoughts always end here, and everything is constantly left unsolved.

Personality

Too quiet for the extroverts, yet noisy for the introverts, she flunctuates between energetic and pinagsakluban ng langit at lupa . Gianna easily talks to new people, but finds it hard to build actual connections with them as she often avoids telling them about her personal life.

Due to her constantly equating her self-worth to her academic performance, she has a strong work ethic. Her classmates attribute her high grades to her superior intellect, but they are surely and utterly mistaken.
