1. My childhood vacation home (as in us pamangkins going there to sleep over during vacation) is a subdivision really near Southville. Southville for me has always been a wall with pretty school uniforms. You have to realize that on the outside, SISC looks like the Great Wall of China. What's inside is a mystery (and probably more expensive than all of my organs combined!).

2. As part of a family in the mid-middle income class, going to college while my sister is also studying college and my younger sister in the Special Education system, looking for funding is difficult. All the State Universities I have applied to rejected me. I was about to give up college until the Department of Science and Technology gave me a chance. They gave me a scholarship! But in one condition: the course must be Level III or up FAAP Accreditation. The only schools near me with that level is SDCA in Bacoor and SISC in Las Pinas. I've had enough studying in small private schools (cheap priv schools, around 15k a year tuition fee) with no laboratories and sub-par teachers, so I decided to take a leap of faith. I've decided to enroll here.
